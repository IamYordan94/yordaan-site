Where it runs

Every edition of the festival has been held on the water of the old centre — the canal belt and the Amstel waterfront. The exact stretch changes year to year and is announced with the route, so treat any map claiming otherwise, including ours above, as a placeholder.

Walking it

Walking costs nothing and needs no booking. Start after dark — through December that means any time after five. A slow loop along the main canals takes about two hours at an Amsterdam pace, longer when a work stops you. Expect the bridges over the main canals to be the busy spots; the side canals are where the walk feels like a walk again.

What you'll see

Light art is placed on the water, on bridges and along the quays — some of it built specifically to be seen from the water side, which is exactly why people book the boats. From the bank you see plenty. From a boat you are inside the exhibition instead of walking around it.
