The festival

Every winter the canals become the museum

Fifteen editions in, the festival runs 26 November 2026 – 17 January 2027. Light works are placed on the water, the bridges and the quays of the old centre, and the city walks and sails past them. The line-up, the theme and the exact route are published closer to the opening, so we state only what is known and label everything else as ours.

Walking the route is free and always open. The works are made to be seen from the water — low, slow, moving through the light instead of past it.
