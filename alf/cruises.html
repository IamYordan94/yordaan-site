Choosing · no museum, just the boats

Which cruise should you book?

Three kinds of boat run this festival: a heated glass-roof boat, an open sloep under the sky, and a historic salon. They are not interchangeable — here is how to choose in about a minute, and then all seven we track are one page away.

Dates, times & pricesWhich cruiseThe route on footPractical answers

Covered

The glass-roof boat

You sit inside, warm, under a glass roof — the light comes to you through the ceiling and the windows. In January, in rain, with children or grandparents, this is the boat.

The catch: everyone books it, so it fills first on weekends.

Open boat

The sloep

Low on the water, no roof, the closest you can get to the works — engines are usually electric and quiet, and most hand out blankets. It is also the coldest way to see the festival, and the one people remember.

The catch: dress for it. Standing still in January on water is colder than it sounds.

Salon

The historic salon boat

A classic saloon hull, inside with a table and a glass, fewer seats, higher price. Comfortable and unhurried — the choice when the evening is the point rather than the boat count.

The catch: the most expensive, and the fewest departures.

What actually differs between offers

Strip away the marketing and a Light Festival cruise varies in five ways:

  • Length — an hour to an hour and a half on the water.
  • What's included — some are unlimited drinks and a snack, some are dry. Our booking page says which is which.
  • A guide or not — a live guide talking you through the works, or nobody, and you look.
  • The crowd — small boat or full boat; both leave with the same lights above them.
  • The price — from €16.99 to €52 as we track them, and the difference is mostly the boat and the drinks.

Check before you pay

Three things decide whether the evening works, and all three sit on the operator's own page: the departure dock (each operator uses its own — a noon-printed address beats a guess), what the ticket includes (drinks, blankets, guide), and the cancellation and exchange terms.
