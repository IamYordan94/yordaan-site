The festival · 15th edition

When it runs, and what it costs

The 15th edition of the Amsterdam Light Festival runs from 26 November 2026 to 17 January 2027 — every evening, Christmas and New Year included. Walking the canals costs nothing. Seeing the works from the water is what the tickets are for.

What it costs

Walking is free. A canal cruise is the only ticket the festival has, and the price is set by whichever operator runs the boat — the cruises we track currently run from €16.99 to €52 a person, depending on the boat, what's included and the time of night. Everything in between is on the booking page with the live price each platform shows.

No fee exists here. This site takes nothing and charges nothing: you pay the operator or the platform, on their page, at their price.

The lights, and when they are on

The festival has not yet published the switch-on and switch-off times for the 2026/27 edition — they arrive with the programme, closer to the opening. What is certain: it is dark in Amsterdam by half past four through December, so any evening walk after five is a walk in the dark, and the cruise departures we track run from 16:00 to 22:15.

Which night to pick

Our take, from the way the city fills up: weekends and the week between Christmas and New Year are the busiest the canals get all year. A midweek evening in the first half of December is the quietest — same lights, less crowd on the bridges and more room on the boats.

Questions we get

Is the Light Festival free?
Yes for walking. The works stand along the canals and the walking route costs nothing — you only need a ticket to see them from the water, on a canal cruise.
How long does it run, and is it on every night?
The 15th edition runs from 26 November 2026 to 17 January 2027, every evening of that period, holidays included. Individual boats keep their own schedules — the live date row on the booking page is the truth for a specific night.
What time do the lights go on?
The festival has not published its switch-on and switch-off times for the new edition yet — they are announced with the programme. The cruises we track currently offer departures from 16:00 to 22:15, and it is dark by half past four through December.
