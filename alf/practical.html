The questions

Is it too cold to enjoy?
It is a winter festival, so dress for standing outside at night: coat, hat, gloves, shoes that keep water out. Covered boats are heated; open boats usually hand you a blanket; nothing about the cold is a surprise.
Can we bring children?
Nothing we have seen bans children from these cruises, but each operator sets its own rules and some apply minimum ages to particular boats — the line that applies is on their booking page. With small children, the covered boat is the comfortable choice.
Can we bring a dog?
Operator by operator. Some allow dogs, some don't — check the operator's page before you book rather than after.
Are drinks included?
Some cruises include unlimited drinks and a snack, others are dry. Each card on our booking page shows what is included, and the operator's page has the detail.
Are there toilets on board?
Small open boats usually have none; larger covered and salon boats usually do. It is stated on the operator's page for the boat you are looking at.
Wheelchair, or difficulty walking?
Glass-roof boats generally board with the fewest steps, but docks differ — tell the operator when you book, or contact them directly if it matters, and ask which of their departures is easiest to board.
What if it rains?
Boats sail in rain. A covered boat is dry; an open one is not, which is part of its charm until it isn't. Cancellations for weather are the operator's decision and their policy — that is on their page.
When is it busiest?
Weekends and the week between Christmas and New Year. Midweek earlier in December is the quietest the festival gets.
Is it worth paying for, when walking is free?
They are two different evenings. Walking is lovely and free. From the water, you are in the middle of the light instead of looking across at it, and that is what the ticket buys. If you do it once, and January is doing its thing, take the covered boat.
Where do the boats leave from?
Each operator has its own dock, and the exact departure point is on the booking page for the boat you choose. We deliberately do not print docks on this site: they differ between operators, and a wrong address is worse than none.
How long is the cruise?
Most of the cruises we track run sixty to ninety minutes on the water, plus boarding time.
