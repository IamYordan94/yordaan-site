03

The invoicing tool

A finished shift should become an invoice line, not something retyped from a calendar at the end of the month. The tool reads a work schedule, lets the operator record the days worked, and produces the invoice as a PDF. It can store those PDFs in the operator's own Google Drive and send them from the operator's own Gmail account.

It is in development. There are no customers and nothing to sign up for. It runs on a single account — mine — with sign-ups switched off, and it exists because the work described above needed it. If that ever changes, it will say so here.

The tool asks for access to Google Drive, Google Calendar and Gmail, and uses that access only to store invoice documents, read work schedules and send invoices. What it covers, and what it does not, is set out in the privacy policy.
