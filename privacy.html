2. What this website does and does not do

This website is a small set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads no advertising or tracking scripts, and loads no fonts or files from third-party servers. There is no contact form, no newsletter sign-up and no comment function; nothing you do on these pages is profiled or stored by me.

One thing is outside my control: the pages are delivered to your browser by a hosting provider and a content delivery network. Those providers process technical data — such as your IP address, the requested URL, the time of the request and your browser's user agent — in order to deliver the page and to protect the service against abuse. This happens on the basis of a legitimate interest in operating the site securely (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. When you email or call

If you email or telephone about a job, a quote or an invoice, I receive your name, contact details and whatever you choose to write. That data is used only to answer you, to quote, and — where a job follows — to carry out the work and keep the administration. The legal bases are taking steps at your request before a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and my legitimate interest in answering business correspondence (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. The invoicing tool and Google account access

I am building an invoicing tool for Dutch freelancers. It is in development and is not offered to the public; there are no customers using it. This section explains what the tool does with a Google account, because that is the part of this business that touches someone else's data directly.

When a user chooses to connect a Google account, the tool asks Google for permission to send email on that user's behalf, using Google's Gmail API. In Google's own terminology this is the send permission (the gmail.send scope). Practically, that means:

  • The tool can compose and send an email — in this case the invoice the user has just created — from that user's own Gmail address.
  • The tool cannot read, search, list or delete messages in the mailbox. Reading the inbox is a separate Google permission, and it is not requested.
  • The tool does not use Google account data for advertising, profiling, or anything other than sending the message the user asked it to send.
  • The user can withdraw the permission at any time in their Google account settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once withdrawn, the tool can no longer send on that account's behalf.

Invoice data that a user enters — client name, amounts, VAT, invoice lines — belongs to that user. It is used to produce and send the invoice and to keep the record the user needs for their administration. It is not sold, not used to train anything, and not disclosed to anyone other than the recipients the user has chosen. Google's own handling of data passing through its API is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; the tool's use of that data is additionally limited by the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

If you have connected an account and want the data associated with it deleted, email Yordancreatives@gmail.com from that address and it will be removed.

6. How long data is kept

Correspondence about a job is kept for as long as the working relationship lasts and afterwards only as long as needed for the administration. Invoices sent and received, and the records behind them, are kept for seven years, because Dutch tax law requires a business to retain its administration for that period (Article 52 of the Algemene wet inzake rijksbelastingen). Data that has no such obligation attached to it is deleted when it is no longer needed.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see the personal data held about you, have it corrected or completed, have it deleted, have its use restricted, object to its use, or receive it in a portable format. Send the request to Yordancreatives@gmail.com; a reply follows within one month, and identification may be requested to make sure data is not handed to the wrong person.

If you think your data is being handled improperly you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl). Raising it with me first is usually quicker.

8. Security

This site is served over HTTPS. Access to the systems holding correspondence and invoice data is limited to the owner. No system is perfectly secure, but the amount of personal data held here is deliberately small: there is no customer database on this website and no login on this website.

9. Changes to this policy

When something changes — for example when the invoicing tool becomes available to other users, or a provider is added — this page is updated and the version date at the top changes. The current version is always the one published at yordaan.com/privacy.html.
